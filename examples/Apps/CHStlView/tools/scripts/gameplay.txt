# The README's one picture (docs/gameplay.gif): the intro, then clips of the
# card list, a model spinning, the modes, turning and zooming, and a big
# model drawn as a draft while it turns. Each clip is its own recording,
# named in the order it is shown; `chgame gif` runs this script and joins
# them. The card is the samples' (tools/make_card.py).
rec start 3
wait 96
rec stop 01_intro
# Into MODELS, down to the gear.
rec start 3
tap DOWN 4
wait 6
tap A
wait 12
tap DOWN 4
wait 4
tap DOWN 4
wait 4

wait 12

# The gear opens spinning; SELECT shows only the faces towards you.
rec start 3
tap A
wait 110

wait 90

# Turned with the D-pad, zoomed in with A, the view reset with A + B.
rec start 3
hold UP
wait 24
release
hold LEFT
wait 30
release
hold A
wait 40
release
wait 10
hold A+B
wait 4
release
wait 50
rec stop 04_turn

wait 40
release
wait 4
tap DOWN 4
wait 4
tap DOWN 4
wait 4
rec start 3
tap A
wait 30
hold RIGHT
wait 50
release
wait 40
